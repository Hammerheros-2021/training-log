Privacy promises

Opt-in, minimal and logged

Signals, not secrets

Coaches see a status and a reason category, never the data behind it.

The member holds the key

Every share is switched on by the member, per person and per area, and can be switched off at any time.

Least access, including Keel

Keel staff see business data. A member’s record opens only with their permission, for a limited time, and is logged.

Underage teams

A parent or guardian approves any sharing; Ireland’s digital age of consent is 16. Underage coaches see availability only.

Never sold

Member data is never sold or used for advertising.

Rights on request

Members can see, export or delete their data, answered within one month.

Health and wellbeing data is special-category data under GDPR, so sharing is opt-in, minimal and logged.
