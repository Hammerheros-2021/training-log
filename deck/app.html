The idea

A walk counts. So does an early night.

Keel is a lifestyle app for everyone, whether you train six days a week or want to move a bit more. It is not another tracker: it reads how you are and suggests what would suit today.

For everyone

Fits round work, family, farm or shifts. A plan for your goals, not an athlete’s.

Food first

Teaches you about food in plain words. Real food before supplements.

Yours alone

Everything you log is private to your account. Your data is never sold.

Coach’s overview

3 things worth knowing

1

Your balance dips the day after long hours of farm work. An earlier night on those days would help.

2

Protein is on target most days. Fibre is the gap: beans, oats or an extra veg at dinner would close it.

3

Three short nights before last Saturday’s game. Protect Thursday and Friday sleep this week.
